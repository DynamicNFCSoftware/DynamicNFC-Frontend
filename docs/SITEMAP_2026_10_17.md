## 6. Navigation audit — the positioning defect

**Navbar (`Navbar.jsx`), logged out:** Home · Industries ▾ · NFC Cards · Contact Sales · [Live Demo] · [✦ AI Tap] · lang · [Login]

**Navbar, logged in:** the same, **plus** two "My Card" links (mobile shortcut line 105, menu line 173) pointing to `/dashboard`, plus email, plus ADMIN badge if admin, plus Logout.

There is **no `/unified` link anywhere in the Navbar, in either state.**

**Login flow:** `Login.jsx` lines 198, 207, 240 — all three auth paths (`navigate('/dashboard')`) land on the business-card manager.

### Consequence

| | Digital business card | Sales Velocity Engine |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial role | free giveaway / lead magnet | the product being sold |
| Post-login landing | **yes — always** | never |
| Navbar link when logged in | **two** | zero |
| Reachable from the other | n/a | only via a public demo gateway |

A prospect who signs up, or a client who logs in, sees "My Cards" and nothing else. The paid surface is reachable only by typing `/unified` or going back out to a public demo page. The free side-gift occupies the product's front door.

### Decided fix (Oguzhan, 2026-10-17) — `CURSOR_DIRECTIVE_PRODUCT_FRONT_DOOR_2026_10_17.md`

1. `/dashboard` becomes `/unified/my-card`, a tab in the Unified sidebar (last but one, under System).
2. Login lands on `/unified`. Legacy `/dashboard` stays as a redirect so printed QR codes survive.
3. Navbar, logged in: one primary link — **Dashboard → `/unified`**. Both "My Card" links removed.
4. `CardDashboard` is **restyled** into the `ud-` system as `MyCardTab.jsx` (+ `MyCardTab.css`,
   prefix `udmc-`), not ported as-is. Its 5 hardcoded English strings move into a 5-language `UI` object.
5. Pricing goes sales-led in a separate PR — `CURSOR_DIRECTIVE_PRICING_SALES_LED_2026_10_17.md`.

**Sequencing:** both branches cut from `main` *after* `feat/yacht-landing` merges — it touches
`App.jsx` and `Home.jsx`, and the front-door work touches `App.jsx` and `Navbar.jsx`.
